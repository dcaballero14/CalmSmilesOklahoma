Looking for a SoonerCare dentist in Oklahoma?

Calm Smiles Oklahoma helps patients search Oklahoma dental clinics by SoonerCare, major insurance plans, location, and comfort features for dental anxiety.

Search clinics that list SoonerCare

Always confirm before scheduling.

Insurance participation can change. Call the clinic and ask whether they currently accept your SoonerCare plan and whether the specific service you need is covered.

What to ask when you call

Coverage

“Do you currently accept SoonerCare / Oklahoma Medicaid for new dental patients?”

Services

“Is the service I need covered, and do I need a referral, prior authorization, or exam first?”

Anxiety support

“I’m anxious about dental care. Can the appointment move slowly, with explanations and breaks?”

Cost clarity

“Is there anything I may need to pay out of pocket, and can you explain that before treatment?”

How to use the directory

  1. Open the main Calm Smiles Oklahoma search page.
  2. Select your Oklahoma region or search by city.
  3. Turn on the SoonerCare filter.
  4. Add anxiety-friendly comfort features that matter to you.
  5. Call the clinic to confirm insurance, services, and availability.

The directory is meant to make the first step easier, but it is not a guarantee of coverage. Confirm details directly with the dental office.
