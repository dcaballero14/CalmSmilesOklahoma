Questions for OKC patients

Anxiety support

“Do you work with patients who are nervous or embarrassed about dental care?”

Cost

“Can you explain the first-visit cost and any estimate before treatment starts?”

Sedation

“Do you offer nitrous or sedation, and what would I need to do before using it?”

Insurance

“Do you accept my plan or SoonerCare, and are you currently taking new patients?”
