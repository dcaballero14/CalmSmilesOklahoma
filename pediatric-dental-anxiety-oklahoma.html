Helping a child with dental anxiety in Oklahoma.

If your child is scared of the dentist, you can ask about pediatric experience, sensory needs, slower appointments, parent presence, and insurance before scheduling.

Search pediatric listings

A calmer first visit can help.

For some kids, the first goal is simply meeting the team, seeing the room, and learning that breaks are allowed.

Questions parents can ask

Experience

“Do you regularly see anxious children or kids who need a slower appointment?”

Parent involvement

“Can I stay with my child during the visit, and how do you explain procedures to kids?”

Sensory support

“Can we bring headphones, a comfort item, sunglasses, or take breaks if needed?”

Coverage

“Do you accept SoonerCare, Children’s Medicaid, or our dental plan for this appointment?”

Before the appointment

  • Tell the office what has been hard for your child in the past.
  • Ask whether the first visit can be low-pressure and explanation-focused.
  • Bring insurance information and any relevant medical or sensory notes.
  • Use simple language with your child: “The dentist will count your teeth and help keep them strong.”

Confirm all services, coverage, and accommodations directly with the clinic.
