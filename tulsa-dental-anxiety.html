Ask before you book.

Call the clinic and ask how they support anxious patients before scheduling. A quick phone call can reduce a lot of uncertainty.

Useful questions for Tulsa patients

Comfort

“Do you work with anxious patients, and can I use a stop signal during treatment?”

Insurance

“Do you accept my plan, SoonerCare, or Oklahoma Medicaid for the treatment I need?”

First visit

“Can the first appointment be just an exam, X-rays, and a conversation about options?”

Cost

“Do you offer payment plans, membership plans, sliding scale fees, or lower-cost options?”
