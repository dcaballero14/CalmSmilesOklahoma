SoonerCare participation can change. Ask the office to verify your eligibility, plan, appointment availability, and expected out-of-pocket costs.

What to ask a Tulsa dental office

Coverage

“Do you currently accept SoonerCare / Oklahoma Medicaid for new dental patients?”

Age limits

“Do you accept SoonerCare for adults, children, or both?”

Services

“Is the treatment I need covered, and do I need an exam or prior authorization first?”

Anxiety support

“Can the appointment move slowly with explanations, breaks, and a stop signal?”

Best next step

Use the directory to narrow your Tulsa options, then call two or three offices. If a clinic is not accepting SoonerCare patients right now, ask whether they know another Tulsa office that is.
