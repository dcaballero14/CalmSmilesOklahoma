What Norman patients should ask before booking

Anxiety support

“Do you routinely work with patients who are nervous, embarrassed, or have avoided dental care?”

Control

“Can we use a stop signal, and can I take breaks if I start to panic?”

Insurance

“Do you accept my dental plan, SoonerCare, or Oklahoma Medicaid for the care I need?”

Comfort options

“Do you offer nitrous oxide, sedation, headphones, music, or a calmer appointment time?”
